TERMS & PRIVACY · 7 NOVEMBER 2026

A thoughtful place to learn.

This is a university group project for language learning. By creating an account, you agree to use it respectfully and follow these terms.

Your account

Use an email you control and keep your password and reset links private. Do not access other learners’ accounts or attempt to bypass access controls. Google sign-in is optional.

Learning content

Stories and vocabulary may be generated by AI and can contain mistakes. Review important translations with a teacher. Do not enter sensitive personal information into story topics. Do not generate harmful or unlawful content.

Information we store

We store your name, email, password hash (for password accounts), Google account identifier (when applicable), stories, vocabulary, quiz answers and learning progress. We store hashes of session and reset tokens. The browser stores your display profile and last story; the session token stays in the current tab’s session storage until logout or tab closure.

External services

Google handles Google authentication. When AI is enabled, story settings, story context and vocabulary are sent to the configured Gemini service. The configured email provider processes recovery emails. Browser speech features may use your browser or operating system’s speech service. These services have their own policies.

Access and retention

Administrators can view account names, email addresses, roles and aggregate usage counts. Learning data remains in the project database until the project team removes it. Contact your course project team through your class channel to request correction or deletion of your account and associated data.

Availability and updates

This educational demo is provided as available, without a service availability guarantee. The project team may update these terms; review the date above. These terms are a project notice and should be reviewed by the team before a public release.
